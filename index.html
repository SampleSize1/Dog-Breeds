Learn every dog breed

387 breeds: every breed the FCI recognises, every AKC breed, and a few common ones only the United Kennel Club lists. Start with Popular (60), then the AKC’s 206, then Every breed.

  1. Study the photos and what to look for, with the breeds each one is mixed up with.
  2. Practice naming the breed in a photo. The wrong choices are its look-alikes, and the ones you miss come back.
  3. Test yourself on every breed in your selection, once each, against the clock.

Picking is easier than typing: switch Answer by in Settings when you’re ready. Crosses (Labradoodle, Cavapoo…) aren’t breeds: there you name the two parents.

Photos come from Wikimedia Commons and Flickr, each checked by eye, and load while you play. Your progress stays in this browser.
